## 1. Executive Summary

Orbitcrew is sold as **a small AI office for a small business**, not as an "agent orchestration platform." The page has one job: get a 10–50 person services firm to request an office. It says four things, in order:

1. **What you get:** a first hire that never needs onboarding.
2. **What it does:** the everyday work across the whole business, from sales and finance to logistics and HR.
3. **Why you can trust it:** it asks you before anything goes out, and every action is on record.
4. **How you start:** request your office. We set it up and run it for you.

**Copy rules from Vision v2.0**
- No internal names on the page: no Paperclip, Hermes, OpenClaw, engines, tiers or modules.
- No "Sign up." Use "Request your office." There is no public self-signup.
- Include the line "Your own instance, your own domain, run for you."

## 4. Design Notes

- **Layout:** two columns on desktop (message left, demo card right). One column on tablet and phone.
- **No scrolling:** the page fills the screen height. A built-in fit check hides detail in this order until everything fits: trust points, the button note and cost line, the team activity list, the subhead, the time stamp. The headline, tabs, approval box and button always stay.
- **Font:** Inter (weights 400–800).
- **Colors:** cool blue-grey background, deep navy text, blue accent. Amber marks "needs approval." Green marks "approved." Light and dark modes both work.
- **Tabs on phones:** ten small tabs wrap onto three or four rows.
- **Motion:** the demo card fades in once. Buttons respond to clicks. Reduced-motion settings are respected.
- **Memorable element:** the approval box. The visitor feels the "you stay in control" promise by clicking it.

## 6. Scope Check: Page vs. Product

PRD v5.1 builds only the **lead response office** (Sales) for launch. Vision v2.0 puts the customer support office in Phase 2, driven by what pilots ask for. Every other department is not in the PRD yet.

| Tab | Status in PRD v5.1 / Vision v2.0 |
|---|---|
| Sales | Launch office (customer zero, October 2026) |
| Support | Second starter team, Phase 2 |
| Marketing, Finance, Procurement, Operations, Logistics, HR, Legal, IT | Not specified |

**Options**
1. **Keep all ten tabs.** The org-chart model lets a customer hire other teammates, so the page shows the direction of the product. Say "starting with sales" on every sales call.
2. **Add a "Coming soon" tag** to every tab except Sales. This is the most honest option and costs little.
3. **Show fewer tabs** (for example Sales, Support, Finance, Operations) until pilots ask for more.

**Risks to review before promoting**
- Finance, Procurement and Logistics demos show a person approving a payment or an order. PRD approval rules treat payments and commitments as the highest approval level, so the demos fit the model, but these offices need building first.
- HR and Legal demos involve hiring and contract decisions. Keep the wording as "drafts for you to approve," and have a lawyer check the page before public promotion.
