## Problem Statement
Owners of 10–50 person professional-services firms answer inbound lead emails themselves, "when free", often hours or a day later. Slow replies lose deals. The owner will not hand the reply to an unsupervised AI.

## Demand Evidence
- One firm asked for this and offered to pay. What it asked for was **lead replies**, which matches the launch wedge.
- **Gap:** the firm's name, the price and its exact words were not given in this session. Until named with a price, this is an anecdote, not evidence.

## Status Quo
- The owner replies to leads personally when they have time.
- The cost is lost deals from delay. It is unquantified: no reply-delay or lost-lead numbers yet.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
- **Person:** the owner-operator who sells, delivers and answers leads. The owner is setup user, approver, Leader and buyer at once.
- **Wedge:** an inbound lead gets an instant template acknowledgment. A researched, on-brand full reply waits on the owner's phone. One tap sends it in the thread, with a receipt.

## Constraints
- Solo founder-developer. Customer zero is due October 2026, and 5–10 pilots November–December 2026.
- Approved today: Node 24 (E1), git + CI (E2), confirm-page approval links (C2/E3), Postgres escalation rows (E4), approval-ID send states (E5), one Postgres server per instance (E6). Also a per-customer own server (C3), a true data promise naming AI providers (C1), the Orbitcrew brand (C8), and time-to-sent-reply measurement (C4).

## Recommended Approach
**C: prototype that becomes the Front Desk.**
- **One ORBIT program per instance** (Node 24, Postgres) using the PRD's real data model: the `events` table (DAT-1), approval-ID states `issued → sending → sent / failed / void` (E5), and receipts as projections of events.
- **Flow:**
  1. Poll Gmail; dedupe by Message-ID (C9); rule filters (FD-1).
  2. Claude decides "lead or not".
  3. **Instant template acknowledgment**, sent under the standing template approval and recorded as its own receipt.
  4. Claude drafts the full reply: firm website, about 20 past sent emails for tone, a "facts we can state" file.
  5. An email link opens the confirm page (the approved wireframe, E3).
  6. The owner taps Send, and the reply goes out in the original thread.
  7. A receipt records the arrival, draft, tap and send timestamps.
- **Rollout:**
  - The founder's inbox (customer zero).
  - Then the named firm on **its own small server**: same code, own Postgres, own domain (C3).
- **Deferred until needed:**
  - Paperclip + Hermes (decision #14 timing): add them when drafts need multi-step research or a second teammate.
  - Multi-role approvals (premise 2).
  - Fleet console (R2).

## Launch behavior rules (proposed defaults; founder confirms at doc approval)

**Acknowledgment (auto-ack) — when it may go out**
- Auto-ack goes out only when **all** of these hold:
  - Claude classifies the email "lead" with confidence ≥ 0.8.
  - The sender is not a known contact: they have never emailed the owner or been emailed by the owner.
  - The message is the first in a new thread.
- Never ack when any of these apply:
  - an `Auto-Submitted`, `Precedence: bulk/list` or `List-*` header;
  - a `no-reply`/`noreply` sender;
  - mail sent by the system itself (every system email carries a hidden `X-Orbitcrew` header and the `Orbitcrew` Gmail label, and both are excluded from polling).
- At most **one ack per sender per 30 days**. At most **10 acks per hour**.
  - Hitting the cap pauses auto-ack and emails the owner.
  - Auto-ack resumes automatically when the rolling hour has fewer than 10 acks.
  - Leads that arrive during the pause get no late ack. They still get a draft and a confirm link, and are listed in the digest as "not acked (cap)".
- Emails classified "not a lead" or "unsure" appear in a daily digest to the owner, so a missed lead is visible within 24 hours.
- **Before auto-ack is switched on**, the 0.8 threshold is calibrated against a labeled replay set: about 50 past leads plus about 50 non-leads from the founder's inbox, plus the firm's forwarded emails. The observed false-positive rate is recorded. Auto-ack stays off until that rate is 0 on the replay set.

**Classifier output → action**

| Case | Ack | Draft + confirm link | Digest |
|---|---|---|---|
| "lead", confidence ≥ 0.8, unknown sender, first message in new thread | yes | yes | — |
| "lead", confidence < 0.8 | no | yes | "lead, not acked (low confidence)" |
| "lead" from a known contact, or not the first message in its thread | no | yes | "lead, not acked (known contact / existing thread)" |
| "unsure" or Claude failed | no | no | "unsure" |
| "not a lead" | no | no | "not a lead" |

**Scope of a thread:** the launch handles only the **first inbound message** of a lead thread. Later messages in that thread (e.g. the lead answering the owner) are left to the owner in Gmail. They are never classified, drafted or acked.

**Acknowledgment — template**
- Fixed text with exactly two merge fields: `{lead_first_name}` and `{owner_calendar_link}`. No other variable content, and **no model output ever goes into the ack**.
- `{lead_first_name}` is taken deterministically from the first word of the From display name. It is used only if it is letters only, 2–20 characters, and not a generic word ("accounts", "info", "admin", "sales", "team", "office"). Otherwise the ack uses "there".
- Content rules: no advice, no pricing, no commitments (including no promise of a reply), no claim of a client relationship. Its shape is "Thanks, {lead_first_name}, we received your message. If it's easier, you can book a time here: {owner_calendar_link}."
- If a draft for an acked lead is discarded or becomes void, the digest lists it as "acked, no full reply". The owner must explicitly choose "replied elsewhere" or "no reply needed".
- The template is stored as a versioned record. Any wording change needs owner re-approval. Each ack receipt names the template version it used.
- For the named firm, the owner's written sign-off on the wording is a go-live requirement.

**Sending (ack and full reply)**
- Both messages are sent from the owner's Gmail address as replies in the lead's original thread: `threadId` plus `In-Reply-To`/`References` headers.

**Confirm page**
- Actions:
  - **Send**: `issued → sending → sent`.
  - **Edit then Send**: the edited text is sent. The receipt stores both draft and final text, and the edit counts toward the edit rate.
  - **Discard**: `issued → void`.
- Timeouts: a reminder email after 2 hours if untouched. The draft becomes `void` after 72 hours, and the lead then appears in the daily digest as "unanswered".
- Before any send, the thread is re-checked. If the owner replied directly or the lead wrote again, the page shows "This thread changed" with the new message, and the owner must confirm again or re-draft (C9).
- The link is signed, single-use, expires with the draft, and is bound to one approval ID. It opens a confirm page and acts only on a button press (E3).
- **Send** of the unmodified draft works from the signed link. **Edit then Send requires the owner to be signed in** (an existing session, or sign-in first). A forwarded link can therefore send only the pre-reviewed draft, never arbitrary text.

**Failures and latency**
- Gmail is polled every **30 seconds** (Gmail push via Pub/Sub is a later upgrade).
- **Latency budget at the 95th percentile (sums to 90 s, under the 2-minute target):**
  - up to 30 s waiting for the poll;
  - 10 s fetch plus known-contact lookup;
  - 15 s Claude timeout;
  - one 15 s retry;
  - 20 s to send the ack.
- If the Claude call fails twice: send no ack, mark the email "unsure" and email the owner an alert.
- **Idempotency:** every system-sent email carries `X-Orbitcrew-Id: <approval ID>` (or `<ack receipt ID + template version>` for acks). A failed send retries at most twice. Before each retry, the Sent folder is searched for that **specific ID**, and a match counts as `sent`. After two retries it becomes `failed`, and the owner gets a notification with a "Resend" button.

**Data sent to the AI provider**
- The tone samples are **20 sent emails the owner selects** at setup, with client-matter emails excluded. No bulk mailbox import.

## Success Criteria
Counting "real leads": during customer zero the founder labels every inbound email weekly (lead / not lead). Lead metrics use that labeled set, so leads the classifier missed still count.

- **Customer zero, 2 weeks:**
  - ≥ 95% of labeled real leads acknowledged within 2 minutes.
  - Median lead-arrival-to-tap time under 30 minutes.
  - ≥ 80% of drafts sent (not discarded).
  - ≥ 50% sent without edits.
  - Zero full replies sent without a tap.
  - Zero acks sent to non-leads, known contacts or auto-senders.
- **Sample size:** each live criterion needs at least **10 labeled real leads**. If fewer arrive in 2 weeks, extend customer zero until 10 have arrived. The replay set (about 50 past leads plus about 50 non-leads) measures the classifier's false-positive rate and the unedited-draft rate: the founder reviews the replayed drafts as if approving. Tap time, ack time and the drafts-sent rate come from live leads only.
- **If customer zero misses:**
  - Median tap time over 30 minutes: the full reply needs a faster path (push notification, or a shorter confirm flow) before pilots.
  - Under 50% unedited: drafting (tone samples, facts file) is fixed before the named firm goes live.
  - Ack rate under 95% within 2 minutes: fix polling and latency before pilots.
  - Drafts sent under 80%: review why drafts were discarded and fix drafting or the classifier before pilots.
  - **Any zero-tolerance breach** (an ack to a non-lead, known contact or auto-sender, or a full reply sent without a tap) **switches off auto-ack, or sending, immediately** until the cause is fixed and the replay set passes again.
- **Named firm, 4 weeks:**
  - The pilot runs on its own server at an agreed price.
  - ≥ 80% of drafts sent.
  - The owner says they would be upset to lose it.

**Problem**

**Problem**

The founder is pre-launch and the only demand evidence is one unnamed firm, so the founder's inbox may not hold anywhere near 50 past inbound leads. ### R3-3 — clarity

**Problem**

> Calibration says auto-ack 'stays off until that rate is 0 on the replay set' but does not say what changes if the rate is above 0: raise the 0.8 threshold, change the prompt, or add rules. It also does not say whether a fixed threshold is re-validated after each change. Zero false positives on about 50 non-leads also only weakly bounds the true rate.

### R3-4 — clarity

**Remedy**

**Remedy**

### R3-6 — clarity

**Problem**

**Remedy**

> Name the owner authentication method for the confirm page and its session duration (e.g. Google sign-in restricted to the owner's address).

**Problem**

> Zero-tolerance breaches 'switch off auto-ack, or sending, immediately'. **Remedy**

> State how each breach type is detected and how quickly (e.g. per-ack receipts in the daily digest for the owner to flag, and an automatic check for known-contact or auto-sender acks). Also state whether the switch-off is automatic or manual.

### R3-8 — completeness

**Problem**
